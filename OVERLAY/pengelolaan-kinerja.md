## **System Definition**

**Pengelolaan Kinerja** is an official government system used to manage the performance cycle of Aparatur Sipil Negara (ASN) in educational context such as but not limited to Guru, Kepala Sekolah, and Pengawas Sekolah.

The system facilitates in three main phases: Perencanaan Kinerja, Pelaksanaan Kinerja, and Penetapan Predikat Kinerja.

The system operates within a regulatory and governance framework. Copy MUST reflect: clear authority boundaries, role-based accountability, process transparency, and substantive accuracy (not administrative wording).

The system is collaborative in nature, but authority is structured and must not be blurred in language.

---

## **Primary Users**

* Pegawai (user that will receive Predikat Kinerja)  
* Pejabat Penilai Kinerja (users that will give Predikat Kinerja)  
* Tim Kinerja (users that will help Pejabat Penilai Kinerja give Predikat Kinerja)  
* Users can have two roles at once, for example Pengawas Sekolah can be Pegawai that receive their own Predikat Kinerja, at the same time can be Tim Kinerja that helps Dinas Pendidikan give Predikat Kinerja to Kepala Sekolah

Users are assumed to be:

* Understand institutional structures  
* Be familiar with performance processes  
* Require clarity on roles and authority

DO NOT assume users are technically savvy. DO NOT assume users understand system logic automatically. Authority distinctions must always be explicit.

---

## **Core User Intent**

Users primarily come to the system to:

* Create Perencanaan Kinerja  
* Discuss with Tim Kinerja (TK) or Pejabat Penilai Kinerja (PPK)  
* Give Rekomendasi Persetujuan (for TK) or approve directly (for PPK)  
* Do Pelaksanaan Kinerja as planned  
* Do Pemantauan Kinerja (for TK) or Penilaian Kinerja (for PPK)  
* Give Rekomendasi Predikat Kinerja (for TK)  
* Give Predikat Kinerja (for PPK)

Copy MUST prioritize:

* Role clarity  
* Authority clarity  
* Clear next steps  
* Active collaboration framing

## **Content Behavior Rules (Pengelolaan Kinerja-Specific)**

* DO NOT imply approval, rejection, or eligibility before official validation.  
* DO NOT promise timelines unless explicitly defined by regulation.  
* Always encourage two-way discussion.  
* Avoid passive bureaucracy tone.  
* Avoid over-legalistic wording.  
* Avoid implying automatic approval.  
* Avoid unnecessary confirmation typing requirements.  
* Always distinguish between:  
  * Data availability vs. process completion  
  * System status vs. institutional decision

Examples:

✅ "Perencanaan Anda sedang diperiksai."  
❌ "Perencanaan Anda akan segera disetujui."

### **Authority Boundaries (Critical Rule)**

Copy MUST NOT imply that:

* Tim Kinerja melakukan Penilaian  
* Tim Kinerja menetapkan Predikat Kinerja  
* Tim Kinerja menyetujui Perencanaan

Tim Kinerja hanya:

* Melakukan Pemantauan  
* Memberikan Rekomendasi

Pejabat Penilai Kinerja adalah pihak yang:

* Melakukan Penilaian  
* Menetapkan Predikat Kinerja

Examples:  
✅ "Tim Kinerja memberikan rekomendasi kepada Pejabat Penilai Kinerja."  
 ❌ "Tim Kinerja menetapkan Predikat Kinerja Anda."

## **Empty States**

Empty states MUST:

* State the administrative reason clearly  
* Indicate whether action is required  
* Avoid implying system error unless confirmed

Example:  
"Perencanaan belum selesai. Berikan himbauan kepada Guru untuk menyelesaikan perencanaan agar Pengelolaan Kinerja dapat dilanjutkan."

---

## **Error & Validation Messaging**

* Errors MUST specify whether the issue is:  
  * Data-related  
  * Eligibility-related  
  * System-related  
  * Users-related/phase-related  
* When possible, reference the corrective action without citing internal system logic.

Example:  
"Lengkapi refleksi sebelum melanjutkan ke tahap berikutnya."
